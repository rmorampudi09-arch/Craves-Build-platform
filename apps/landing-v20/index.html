How Craves works

  1. Set your address. Open craves.in/home to see the home kitchens cooking near you right now.
  2. Choose a home chef. Browse fresh dishes and check ratings and reviews before you order.
  3. Pay securely and track your delivery. Follow your order from the chef's kitchen to your door.

Why Craves

Everyday food should still feel personal. We connect you with home chefs who cook with care, familiarity and freshness. Every meal brings you closer to home and supports a real kitchen in your community: real home food, made with care and delivered with trust.

Frequently asked questions

What food can I order on Craves?

Home-style biryani, breakfast and tiffin, lunch and dinner meals, veg and non-veg curries, healthy meals, snacks, sweets and weekly meal subscriptions, all cooked by approved home chefs.

Are Craves home chefs verified?

Every Craves home chef goes through an application and document review before their kitchen can list food, and customers can see ratings and reviews before ordering.

Does Craves deliver in Hyderabad and Bangalore?

Yes. Hyderabad and Bangalore are the primary Craves cities. Set your address on craves.in/home to see which kitchens deliver to you today.

Can I get regular meals instead of ordering every day?

Yes. Craves offers weekly and monthly homemade meal plans from trusted home chefs, including daily lunch and dinner tiffin subscriptions.
