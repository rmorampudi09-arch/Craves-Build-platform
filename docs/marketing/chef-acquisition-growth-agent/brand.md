## 1. About Craves (for chefs)

- **What Craves is.** A homemade food marketplace based in Hyderabad. Home chefs cook in their own home kitchens. Customers order from home chefs near them. Our contact page says: "a Hyderabad-based homemade food marketplace".
- **Where we are right now.** Craves is welcoming home chefs first. Customer sign-up is not open yet. The app tells customers "Craves is launching soon for customers". Our own support guide says: never promise a date.
- **So what we are recruiting for.** Home chefs who want to be among the first on Craves in their area, and set up their kitchen before customers open.
- **City.** Hyderabad only, for now. Owner decision (14 Oct 2026): recruit in Hyderabad now, Bangalore later. Bangalore appears in some website copy, but we do not mention it in our content until the owner says so.
- **Instagram.** @craves.in_ (account id 17841414797291891). 56 followers and 2 posts, both chef recruitment, as of 14 Oct 2026.
- **Where the application starts.** https://craves.in/chef/application. It works whether you are signed in or not. If you are signed out, it asks for your phone number and a one-time code (OTP), then brings you back to the application. Do not send people to craves.in/#for-chefs: signed-in visitors get sent to /home and never see the chef section.
- **What happens after they apply (in the app's own words).**
  1. "Thank you for joining Craves. Your application has been received, and our team will review your details."
  2. Their application gets a reference like CRV-10001, shown on the status page. The reference is given as soon as the application is saved, so a reference does not prove it was submitted.
  3. "Applications are reviewed in the order they arrive, so the time can vary."
  4. If approved: "Once approved, you can add menu items, configure working hours and manage orders."
- **Who to email.** contact@craves.in. The contact page lists it for "home-chef programmes". There is no separate chef recruitment email, WhatsApp or phone line.

## 7. Language guidance

- **Default: Tenglish.** Telugu written in Latin script, mixed with English. Our Tenglish carousel got 189 views against 128 for the Telugu-script image post. That is n=1 for each, and the formats were different (carousel vs single image), so it is a hint, not proof.
- **Variants to test:** Telugu script, and Hindi/Dakhini. Telugu and Urdu are Hyderabad's official languages, the city's Urdu is Deccani (Dakhini), and a large part of the city speaks Hindi. Log the language of every reel in the `language` column of results.csv (`Tenglish`, `Telugu script`, `Hindi/Dakhini`) so LEARN can compare.
- **Gloss rule.** Every Tenglish, Telugu or Hindi/Dakhini line in a script gets an English gloss underneath, in brackets, so the whole team can check it.
  Example (from our first post):
  `Home Chef ga mee journey start cheyyandi.`
  `(Start your journey as a Home Chef.)`
- **Native speaker check.** A native Telugu speaker reads every script aloud before recording. For a Hindi/Dakhini variant, a native Dakhini or Hindi speaker does the same. Claude's Telugu can be wrong or sound bookish.
- **Keep in English, in every language:** Craves, CHEF, FSSAI, OTP, application, craves.in.
- **Spelling.** Tenglish has no fixed spelling. Reuse the spellings from our first post (mee, vantalu, cheyyandi) so we stay consistent.
- **On-screen text in Telugu script.** The new Indian-language fonts Instagram added to Edits cover only Devanagari and Bengali-Assamese, not Telugu. Use CapCut or Canva for Telugu script and check every letter renders before export.
- **Instagram's AI translation and dubbing** supports Telugu (rolled out to everyone from 16 Jan 2026). Do not rely on it for recruitment reels. A real voice in the real language does the job.

## 10. FSSAI facts for scripts

FSSAI is the most common worry, and the most useful explainer we can make. Say these as "FSSAI says..." and show fssai.gov.in or foscos.fssai.gov.in on screen.

| Fact | Source | How to say it |
|---|---|---|
| From 1 Apr 2026, FSSAI **registration** (not a licence) covers food businesses with turnover up to Rs 1.5 crore. It used to be Rs 12 lakh. | FSSAI press release, 13 Mar 2026 | "FSSAI says registration now covers food businesses up to Rs 1.5 crore turnover." |
| Above that, a State licence applies up to Rs 50 crore, and a Central licence beyond. | Same press release | Rarely needed. Most home chefs need registration only. |
| Registrations and licences now have perpetual validity: no periodic renewal. | Same press release | "FSSAI says registrations no longer need renewing. Annual fees still apply: check foscos.fssai.gov.in." |
| Annual fees still apply. | Tax article, 9 Jul 2026 | "Annual fees still apply. Check the current fee on foscos.fssai.gov.in." Never state an amount (N10). |
| For micro and small businesses FSSAI promises "simpler compliance requirements, reduced paperwork and fees, elimination of pre-inspection, and instant registration". | Same press release | Quote it as FSSAI's words. Never promise that a viewer will get it instantly. |
| You apply on FoSCoS: create a user ID, start an application, choose your kind of business, fill Form A (registration), upload self-attested documents, accept the declaration, pay the fee. | NIFTEM guide, 2022 (before the reform) | "The steps are on foscos.fssai.gov.in." ## 11. Compliance

### ASCI basics (the Indian advertising code)

- **1.1 Truthful and provable.** Every fact must be one we can prove if ASCI asks. That is what the ledger is for.
- **1.2 Say the source and date** when a claim comes from research. So: "FSSAI says, March 2026".
- **1.4 No misleading by omission.** "Craves brings the orders" without saying customers open soon would mislead. Whenever we mention orders or customers, we also say customers open soon.
- **1.5 No exploiting inexperience, no claims that end in disappointment.** Many of the people we recruit have never sold food. No income promises.
- **1.6** Exaggeration is fine only when it is obviously a joke.
- ASCI does not fine, but it can escalate cases to the government and regulators.

### Meta policy

- Meta's unrealistic-outcomes policy (for ads, and we apply it to everything) bans claims of getting rich quickly, claims that little or no work is needed to make money, and guarantees of financial gain without details of the role, requirements and risks. It also bans asking for upfront payment or financial account details. So: no income claims, and we always describe what the work involves (cooking, photos, FSSAI, review).
- When we boost or run ads (the 11 to 13 Oct posts were boosted): Meta has an Employment special ad category with targeting limits. It is not confirmed whether a home-chef recruitment ad in India falls under it. Check in Ads Manager when setting up the ad.

### When someone other than @craves.in_ posts about Craves

ASCI requires a clear label when anyone with a connection to Craves posts about it:
- **Who:** a Craves chef, an employee or consultant, a relative of someone at Craves, or anyone given something free or paid.
- **Label:** one of Ad, Advertisement, Sponsored, Collaboration, Partnership, Employee, Free Gift, or Instagram's own paid-partnership tool. English or the language of the post is fine.
- **Where:** upfront, not only in the bio, not at the end, not behind "more", not buried in hashtags. On a Reel, put it inside the video.
- **How long on a Reel:** for videos over 15 seconds and under 2 minutes, at least one-third of the length (about 15 seconds on a 45-second reel). For 15 seconds or less, at least 3 seconds.
- Craves and the person posting share responsibility for the claims, so their post follows this ledger too.

### Consent for real chefs and real kitchens

- Written consent before we show anyone's name, face, voice, kitchen or dishes linked to them. The consent says which content, where it will be posted and for how long, and that they can withdraw.
- Keep a copy of every consent. [CONFIRM WITH OWNER: do we have a consent form, and where are signed copies kept?]
- No consent? Film hands only, and no identifying details.
- Never show children, documents (Aadhaar, PAN, FSSAI certificate), phone numbers or anything from N14 on screen. If you screen-record the application, use a test account and stop before the address and document steps.

### DMs and comments

- Never ask for documents or addresses in DMs or comments (N15). Everything goes inside the application.
- If someone sends a document anyway, don't open or forward it. Reply: "Please upload this inside the application instead, and delete it from this chat." Tell the owner.
- Hide promo-account comments such as "Love this content send this post please". Do not reply to them.
- Instagram allows one private reply per comment, within 7 days of the comment. We can only follow up after they reply, and within 24 hours of their reply.
