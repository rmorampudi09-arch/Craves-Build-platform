## B) Boost plan

The owner boosts. Budget and spend are the owner's call. Nothing here predicts results.

**What to boost, in order**

| Order | Piece | Start | Goal | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C1 launch carousel | Day 1 (Thu 15 Oct), right after it posts | Messages | 7 days |
| 2 | C3 checklist | Day 1 as well (or the day the calendar posts it) | Messages | 7 days |
| 3 | The best reel at 48 hours | 48 hours after the first reel posts | Messages | 3 to 7 days |
| 4 onward | The next best reel not yet boosted | When a reel boost ends | Messages | 3 to 7 days |

**How to pick the reel**

1. At 48 hours, work out CHEF comments per 1,000 views for each reel not yet boosted: keyword_comments ÷ views × 1,000. Use organic numbers only, and leave out team tests.
2. The highest wins. On a tie, or if every reel is still at 0, go down the LEARN order: DM replies, then CRV references, then saves + shares per 1,000 views.
3. Before you press Boost, write that reel's 48-hour numbers into `notes` (see Logging). After the boost starts, the row is no longer organic.

**Before every boost**

- A boosted post is an ad. Meta's ad review and its unrealistic-outcomes policy apply in full (brand.md section 11). The piece must have passed the claims check, with S11 next to every mention of orders, customers or earnings.
- A reel still in trial is shown only to non-followers. Share it to followers first. If the app does not offer Boost on a trial reel, that is probably why (not confirmed).
- Name the person on DMs for every day of the boost.
- Run only as many boosts at once as that person can answer within the hour. This is our suggestion, not data.

**Goal: Messages or Profile visits**

- **Messages (our default).** The boosted post gets a message button, and each result is someone opening a DM with us.
  - The DM person replies with the private DM from week-1.md section 4: the link, the checklist, and the area and dish question. So the conversation ends with the application link, and later we can ask for the CRV reference.
  - This is the closest a boost gets to an application.
  - The cost: a person has to answer each message, and fast. The comment automation does not answer these because they are DMs, not comments.
  - If the boost flow lets you set a greeting, use: "Namaskaram! Craves lo home chef ga apply cheyyadaniki link kavala?" (Hello! Want the link to apply as a home chef on Craves?)
- **Profile visits.** People go to @craves.in_ and see the bio link to the application and the pinned posts. No one needs to staff it.
  - We can't see who taps the bio link (`profile_links_taps` was 0 every day, week-1.md section 1), so these visits can't be tied to applications. The visitor also has two more steps to take.
- **Pick:** Messages on every boost day when someone can answer DMs within the hour. Use Profile visits only on days nobody can. Keep one goal per piece so its results stay readable.

**Audience**

- **Location:** Hyderabad only, and never another city (N17). If the flow asks for a radius, centre it on Hyderabad.
- **Interests:** 3 to 5 of what Meta offers around cooking and home food, such as cooking, recipes, home cooking, Indian cuisine and baking. No interests about jobs, working from home, income or making money (N1, N12).
- **Age:** let Meta suggest it. If the flow shows a minimum age, keep it at 18 or older (S7). Don't narrow by gender.
- **Special ad category:** if Meta classes the boost as Employment (or another special category), accept it and take the targeting limits that come with it. Don't reword the post to avoid the category, and never add job, hiring or salary words (N12).

**Duration:** 3 to 7 days per boost. Budget is the owner's call.

**Logging, so LEARN compares organic with organic**

- In `notes` of results.csv, for every boosted piece: `boosted; goal=messages; 15-21 Oct`. For a reel boosted after 48 hours, first add `organic48h views=N chef=N`.
- Messages that come in through a boost go in `notes` as `boost_msgs=N`. CRV references from them go in that piece's `crv_refs`.
- LEARN ranks rows without `boosted` against each other. Boosted reels enter that ranking only through their organic48h numbers. Boosted rows are compared only with other boosted rows (prompts.md, LEARN notes). C1 and C3 are boosted from day 1, so they have no organic reading.
- Paid results can't be pulled until the Meta Ads connector is added in Windsor.ai (README.md). The owner saves the boost results screen from the app when each boost ends.
- Account-level reach will jump during boosts, as it did on 12 Oct (12,304). Don't read that as organic growth.
