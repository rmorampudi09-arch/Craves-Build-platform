# The five prompts: SCOUT, WRITE, BUILD, TALK, LEARN

These are the weekly prompts for the Craves chef acquisition loop. Paste them into the Claude Project set up in README.md (custom instructions from project-instructions.md, knowledge files brand.md, results.csv and prompts.md). Fill in everything in [square brackets].

**The goal behind every prompt:** home chefs in Hyderabad who submit a Craves chef application. Not customers. Not views for their own sake.

**Two rules that apply to every step:**
- Claims come only from the CLAIMS LEDGER in brand.md. SAFE only. CHECK FIRST stays out until someone checks. NEVER stays out.
- Every Tenglish, Telugu or Hindi/Dakhini line gets an English gloss underneath. A native speaker reads every script aloud before recording (Telugu for Tenglish or Telugu script, Hindi/Dakhini for Hindi/Dakhini).

| Step | Day | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1. SCOUT | Monday | 10 to 15 min |
| 2. WRITE | Monday | 10 min |
| 3. BUILD | Tuesday | 30 to 90 min |
| 4. TALK | Set up once, then every post (Wed to Fri) | 15 min per post |
| 5. LEARN | Sunday | 10 to 15 min |

## Step 1: SCOUT (Monday)

Find what is holding the attention of home cooks in Hyderabad, then work out *why*.

**Where to look (10 minutes in the Instagram app):**
- Home-food sellers in Hyderabad: tiffin makers, biryani and pickle sellers, home bakers.
- Hyderabad food creators who cook home-style food (for example, Telugu home-recipe accounts).
- Side-income and home-business reels aimed at homemakers. Study how they talk, not what they promise: many make income claims we are not allowed to make.
- Any home-food platform recruiting cooks. Our research did not find verified recruitment reels from other platforms, so this needs your eyes.

**What to save:** 5 reels that clearly beat that account's usual views. Note the account's usual views next to each.

> Claude only reads what you paste. Do not use scraping tools: they can break Instagram's terms and put the account at risk. Never download or repost someone else's video. Borrow the *why*, never the footage.

```
SCOUT. Here are [5] reels taking off this week with home cooks and home food in Hyderabad.

Reel [1]
- Account type: [home-food seller / Hyderabad food creator / side-income or home-business / platform recruiting cooks]
- Language: [Tenglish / Telugu script / Hindi/Dakhini / English]
- Hook (first line said or shown): [ ]
- Rough transcript: [ ]
- Views: [ ]   That account's usual views: [ ]
- What's on screen: [ ]
- Comments that stand out: [ ]

[Repeat for reels 2 to 5]

For each reel, tell me:
1. The hook type (result-first, contrarian, POV, curiosity-gap, question, send-to-a-friend). Use these exact labels in the hook_type column of results.csv.
2. Why it held attention past 3 seconds.
3. What made people comment, save or share.
4. What fear or barrier of a home cook it answered (for example: "I can cook but I can't sell", FSSAI and paperwork, family worries, no time, sharing my address, "will anyone even order?"). Use the barrier table in brand.md.

Then give me 3 reel ideas for home chefs in Hyderabad that borrow the WHY, not the content. For each idea give:
- the barrier it answers
- the hook type and language
- the claims it needs, with their ledger numbers from brand.md (S1, S8 and so on)
Drop any idea that needs a CHECK FIRST or NEVER claim, or tell me exactly what someone must check first.
If results.csv has reel rows, say which past reel each idea builds on.
```

## Step 2: WRITE (Monday)

Pick one idea. Claude writes hooks, the script, the shot list and the caption, and checks every claim.

```
WRITE. Use idea #[number].
Language: [Tenglish (default) / Telugu script / Hindi/Dakhini]
Who appears: [hands only / a team member: name / a real chef who has signed written consent: yes or no]

Give me:
1. 5 hook options, each a different hook type, ranked. Mark your pick. For each: the spoken line (said in the first 1.5 seconds) and the on-screen hook text (8 words or fewer). English gloss under each.
2. A voiceover script, 40 to 45 seconds read aloud (about 100 to 120 English words, or the same speaking time in Tenglish), built on the best hook. Give the word count and an estimated read time. One idea only. Each line in [language] with the English gloss underneath in brackets. The last line asks people to comment CHEF.
3. A shot list: one line per beat with the time, what is on screen, the on-screen text, and which voiceover line is being said. Real home kitchen footage, or for an animated explainer (BUILD 3e) brand cards with some real kitchen footage mixed in. No stock, no AI faces. No address, documents, phone numbers or faces without consent on screen.
4. A one-line caption that ends with "Comment CHEF", plus 8 to 10 hashtags from the hashtag bank in brand.md or close to it. No income hashtags.
5. A claims check table for every factual statement in the hooks, script, on-screen text and caption: claim | ledger row | SAFE / CHECK FIRST / NEVER | keep, rewrite or cut. Rewrite or cut anything that is not SAFE.
6. A style check: no earnings talk, no "orders waiting" or "start today", no hype words, no em dashes, no "game-changer". If the reel mentions orders or customers, it must also say customers open soon (ledger S11).
Check everything against brand.md and the best rows in results.csv (highest keyword comments per 1,000 views). Cite the reel you learned from.
```

**Tip:** read the hook out loud. If it doesn't make *you* want to hear the next line, ask for 5 more. Then send the script to the native speaker before Tuesday.

## Step 3: BUILD (Tuesday)

Turn the approved script into the reel. Most reels are phone footage of a real home kitchen. Animated explainers (like the FSSAI reel) can be built in Claude Code.

### 3a. Get a filming checklist from Claude

```
BUILD. Here is the approved script and shot list: [paste the WRITE output, after the native speaker's corrections].
Filming on: [phone model]. Kitchen: [whose kitchen; consent signed: yes or no].

Give me:
1. A shot-by-shot filming checklist for a phone: framing, what to have on the counter, what must NOT be in frame, and how many seconds of each shot to record (record extra).
2. Burned-in subtitle text, line by line, timed to the voiceover, in [language]. Mark the hook text that must be on screen by 1.5 seconds.
3. Voiceover recording notes: pace, pauses, which words to stress.
4. An edit plan for [CapCut / Canva]: order of clips, where the subtitles sit, the end card ("Comment CHEF" plus the Craves logo).
5. A final pre-post check against the CLAIMS LEDGER and the privacy rules in brand.md section 11.
```

### 3b. Phone filming checklist

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Vertical (9:16), back camera, phone on a stand at counter height, clean lens | Sharp, steady, full-screen footage. Instagram shows low-resolution reels less. |
| Daylight from a window, lights on, counter tidy | Real kitchens look good when they are bright and clear. |
| A real home kitchen and real cooking: tadka, rolling, packing a tiffin | Original footage. Instagram does not recommend accounts that mainly post unoriginal content to non-followers (extended from Reels to photos and carousels on 30 Apr 2026). |
| Hands only, unless the person has signed written consent | brand.md section 11. |
| Nothing in frame that shows where someone lives: house number, street, gate, building name, a bill, a letter, a window view of the street | Ledger N14. |
| No documents, phone numbers or children in frame | brand.md section 11. |
| If you film the application on a phone, use a test account and stop before the address and document steps | Ledger N14 and N15. |
| Record the voiceover in a quiet room, phone close to your mouth. Keep a little kitchen sound underneath. | Reels with no sound are shown less. |
| Export without another app's watermark, no borders, not mostly text | Instagram shows watermarked, bordered and text-heavy reels less. |

### 3c. Safe zone, subtitles and voice

- **Safe zone.** Instagram covers the top of the screen with the header and the bottom with the caption and buttons. Keep all text and the main visual in the centre. Use your editor's Instagram Reels safe-zone guide.
- **Subtitles.** Big, burned in, in the reel's language. Plus Jakarta Sans for the hook, Inter for subtitles (or the closest your editor has). White on Flame Red `#F62E18` only when large and bold. Otherwise `#1A1A1A` on white or `#F1F3F5`. For Telugu script, use CapCut or Canva and check every letter.
- **Voice.** Best: a real native speaker from the team. An AI voice in Telugu often sounds off. If you try ElevenLabs, keep tags light (`[warm]`, `[pause]`) and have the native speaker approve it.
- **Length.** 30 to 50 seconds. Hook text on screen by 1.5 seconds. Ends with "Comment CHEF".

### 3d. Post it as a Trial Reel when testing a hook

With 56 followers, reach has to come from non-followers. A Trial Reel is shown to non-followers first. Results arrive after about 24 hours, and you can then share it to followers by hand, or let Instagram do it if it performs.
- One hook per Trial Reel. Write `trial` in the `notes` column of results.csv.
- Post from the Instagram app. The Windsor.ai post action has no Trial Reel option.
- Log the time you post in `post_time_ist`. Test mid-morning (11:00 to 12:30 IST) and evening (20:00 to 21:30 IST). These are guesses for a home-cook audience, not facts.

### 3e. Pro route: animated explainer in Claude Code

Use this for explainers such as "FSSAI in 40 seconds". Open Claude Code in the repo and paste:

```

Script, shot list and subtitles (approved by a native speaker): [paste]

Brand (from docs/marketing/chef-acquisition-growth-agent/brand.md, section 8):
- Flame Red #F62E18. White text on it only when large and bold. Small text on red uses #C92716.
- Text #1A1A1A, light background #F1F3F5, gold #F5B400 for small accents only.
- Plus Jakarta Sans for headlines, Inter for body text.
- For Telugu script, use Noto Sans Telugu (Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter have no Telugu letters) and check every letter renders.
- Logo on the end card only: apps/customer-web-next/public/brand/craves-logo-20260805.svg. Do not redraw or recolour it.

Rules:

- No stock people, no AI faces, no made-up app screens, no numbers that are not in the brand.md ledger.
```

## Step 4: TALK (set up once, then every post)

Every real comment gets a reply. Everyone who comments CHEF gets the application link and an offer of help finishing it.

### Set up (desktop, one time)

1. **Meta Business Suite** on a computer: find the "Comment to message" automation (menu names may differ).
2. **Keywords:** `CHEF`, `Chef`, `chef`, `చెఫ్`. Vendor guides say an automation takes up to 5 keywords and matching is case-sensitive and exact, which is why all four are listed. They also say a keyword can sit in only one automation, and that turning on Meta Business Agent pauses existing automations. These rules are quoted for DM keyword automations. It is not confirmed that comment-to-message works the same way, so test each keyword spelling on the first reel.
3. **Public reply:** paste one public reply from the TALK output (week-1.md section 4 has the first set).
4. **Private message:** paste the private DM from the TALK output.
5. **Test it on the first reel** before relying on it. It is not confirmed whether Meta's comment-to-message automation fires on Reels comments. If it doesn't, send the DM by hand.
6. If you later move to ManyChat or LinkDM, switch the Business Suite automation off first so two tools don't reply to the same comment.

**Instagram's limits (Meta's private replies rules):**
- One private message per comment, sent within 7 days of the comment.
- Follow-ups only after they reply, and within 24 hours of their reply.
- If they don't follow @craves.in_, the DM lands in their Message requests. Say so in the public reply.

**The DM link (always this one):**
`https://craves.in/chef/application?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=dm&utm_campaign=chef_acq`

It works signed in or signed out (signed out goes to phone OTP, then back). Nothing records the `utm_` part today. It is there so attribution works on the day engineering builds source capture on the chef application. Never send `craves.in/#for-chefs`.

### The TALK prompt

```
TALK. My CTA keyword is CHEF (automation keywords: CHEF, Chef, chef, చెఫ్).
The freebie is: the Craves chef application link, plus our help finishing it.
Link: https://craves.in/chef/application?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=dm&utm_campaign=chef_acq
This reel: [hook and topic]. Language: [Tenglish / Telugu script / Hindi/Dakhini].

Write in [language], with an English gloss under every line:
1. 3 public comment replies: short (under 12 words), varied, human. Say the link is in their DMs and to check Message requests if they don't see it. No link, no promises, and do not use the word "chef" (so our replies don't count as CHEF comments).
2. One private DM. This is the only message Instagram lets us send before they reply, so it must do everything:
   - thank them
   - give the link
   - say they apply with their phone number (OTP)
   - say what they need to submit: two kitchen photos, an ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN, bank statement or other government ID) and a 14-digit FSSAI number
   - say they can save and finish later
   - say honestly that customers open soon and chefs are joining first
   - say: upload documents only inside the application, please don't send them here
   - ask them to reply here with their CRV reference (it looks like CRV-10001) after they submit, "so we can help if anything gets stuck"
   - end with the follow-up question from point 3
3. One follow-up question that gets them to reply with their area in Hyderabad (a neighbourhood like Kukatpally, never a street or house address) and their special dish.
4. One reply for after they answer (we have 24 hours): offer help with any step they are stuck on, and remind them to send their CRV reference after they submit, "so we can help if anything gets stuck".

Rules:
- Only SAFE claims from the brand.md CLAIMS LEDGER.
- Never ask for Aadhaar, PAN, FSSAI certificate, bank details or address. Documents go only inside the application.
- If someone sends a document anyway, don't open or forward it. Reply: "Please upload this inside the application instead, and delete it from this chat." Tell the owner.
- No earnings figures and never the fee or commission. If they ask about money, commission, payouts, review time, FSSAI, Bangalore or the app, use the "If someone asks..." answers in brand.md. Don't guess.
```

### In the first hour after posting

- Reply to real questions yourself. It is the most valuable 15 minutes of the week. week-1.md has a reply bank.
- **Hide** promo-account comments like "Love this content send this post please". Do not reply to them.
- Check comments where CHEF is inside a longer comment ("chef please", "I want to be a chef"). The automation may not fire for them, so send the private DM by hand.
- When someone sends a CRV reference, log it against the reel in the `crv_refs` column of results.csv.
- Count DM replies per reel for the `dm_replies` column.

## Step 5: LEARN (Sunday)

This is the step that makes the loop compound. Log each reel 7 days after posting, then run one of the two versions below.

**What LEARN ranks, in order:**
1. **CHEF comments per 1,000 views** (`keyword_comments` / `views` x 1,000). The leading sign of real interest.
2. **DM replies** (`dm_replies`).
3. **CRV references received** (`crv_refs`). Each one is an application we can tie to a reel. Check its status in the admin chef-reviews list before you count it as submitted.
4. **Saves + shares.**
5. **Average watch time vs length, and skip rate** (`avg_watch_s`, `skip_rate`). Skip rate is "the percentage of views from people who skipped during the first 3 seconds of the reel."

Follows are secondary. Instagram's API does not report follows for reels. Read them in the app if you want them.

**Where application counts come from (`applications_7d`):** an admin counts them and sends you the number (marketing does not get Admin Explorer access). The source is Admin Explorer at `/admin/analytics`, chefs dataset, with a date filter. It counts by status. Note: it can include unsubmitted drafts, because a draft row is created once kitchen details are filled in. The chef-reviews list leaves drafts out but has no date filter, so count by submitted date by hand. Count the 7 days after each reel, and the 7 days before it for comparison. Write the before-count in the `notes` column as `prev7d=N` (the column list is fixed, so it goes in notes).

### 5a. LEARN, manual version

Open each reel 7 days after posting, then **View insights**. Fill in its row in results.csv, re-upload results.csv to the Project (replace the old one), then run:

Insights does not show CHEF comments. For `keyword_comments`, count comments that are exactly CHEF, Chef, chef or చెఫ్ (the same rule as 5b; leave out spam and team test comments). Take `dm_replies` and `crv_refs` from your daily log, and `applications_7d` from the admin's daily counts.

```
LEARN. Read results.csv.
Our goal is submitted chef applications from Hyderabad.
Applications in the 7 days before each reel (Admin Explorer): [reel date | count]

1. Rank my reels by CHEF comments per 1,000 views. Break ties by DM replies, then CRV references, then saves + shares, then average watch time vs length and skip rate. Show the table.
2. What do the top 3 have in common? Look at hook type, language, length, topic, the barrier it answered, and posting time (post_time_ist).
3. Where do people drop off? Compare avg_watch_s to length_s, and look at skip_rate.
   - High skip rate: the hook or first 3 seconds need work.
   - Good watch time but few CHEF comments: the ending or the offer needs work.
4. Compare applications_7d for each reel with the 7 days before it was posted. Remember: Admin Explorer counts can include unsubmitted drafts, and overlapping 7-day windows can't be split between reels. Give n for every comparison and say when it proves nothing.
5. Give me 3 rules for next week's reels. Each rule must cite the reel it came from (date and hook).
6. Rewrite next week's hook using those rules: spoken line plus on-screen text (8 words or fewer), in Tenglish with an English gloss. Check it against the CLAIMS LEDGER in brand.md.
If a column is empty, say so. Never guess a number.
```

```
   a. Rank by CHEF comments per 1,000 views, then DM replies, then CRV references, then saves + shares, then avg watch vs length and skip rate.
   b. What the top 3 have in common (hook type, language, length, topic, barrier, posting time).
   c. Drop-off: avg watch time vs length, and skip rate.
   d. applications_7d vs the week before, with the drafts caveat and n for every comparison.

Read only. Do not reply to, hide or post anything through Windsor.ai. If you think a comment should be hidden or answered, list it and wait for my yes on that exact action.
```

**Notes on the data:**
- Reel-only fields (`media_reel_avg_watch_time`, `media_reel_skip_rate`) are empty for feed posts. Our two baseline posts are feed posts.
- Per-reel follows and profile visits are not available for reels through the API.
- Instagram usually does not give follower city or age data below 100 followers, and returns none for our account today, so we cannot yet prove the audience is in Hyderabad. The area question in TALK is our best signal.
- The 11 to 13 Oct posts were boosted (owner confirmed). No Meta Ads connector is connected, so boosted results can't be read. If a reel gets boosted, write `boosted` in `notes` and compare it only with other boosted reels.
